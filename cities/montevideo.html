Montevideo

Elegant colonial houses, neoclassical palaces, art deco landmarks, coastal mansions, and historic theaters shape Montevideo’s refined architectural identity. Strongly influenced by Europe, its cities preserve a balance of sophistication, history, and local character expressed through harmonious urban design.

Facultad de Derecho

A neoclassical landmark in Montevideo, the Facultad de Derecho stands out for its monumental columns and symmetrical design. Built in the early 20th century, it reflects academic prestige and remains a symbol of legal education in Uruguay.

Iglesia de las Carmelitas

Inspired by European Gothic architecture, this church features pointed arches, intricate details, and a serene atmosphere. Its elegant design and spiritual significance make it one of the most distinctive religious landmarks in Montevideo.
