Buenos Aires

Grand civic monuments, neoclassical palaces, neo-Gothic cathedrals, historic theaters, and cultural landmarks define the rich architectural identity of Buenos Aires. Shaped by European influence, political ambition, and artistic vision, its cities preserve elegance, scale, and a powerful sense of national history.

Casa Rosada

The Casa Rosada stands at the heart of Argentina’s political life, its distinctive color marking centuries of power and transformation. Behind its elegant façade lie stories of leadership, conflict, and the shaping of a nation’s identity.

Palacio de Gobierno de la Ciudad

The Palace of the City Government stands as a historic landmark within Buenos Aires’ civic core, reflecting the administrative evolution of the capital. Its presence along Avenida de Mayo connects political tradition with urban identity, while inside, decades of governance have shaped the development and character of the city.

Palacio Paz

The Palacio Paz embodies the grandeur of Argentina’s aristocratic past, a residence born from ambition and refined taste. Its lavish interiors and imposing structure tell a story of wealth, culture, and European influence, preserving an era where elegance and power were closely intertwined.

Teatro Colón

The Teatro Colón rises as one of the world's most revered opera houses, a symbol of artistic excellence in Buenos Aires. Its grand halls, refined ornamentation, and legendary acoustics create an atmosphere where music and architecture merge.

Congreso de la Nación Argentina

The National Congress stands as a monumental expression of democracy, where the nation’s laws and debates take form. Its majestic dome and classical architecture evoke authority and tradition, while inside, the voices of representatives continue to define the country’s evolving path.

Palacio de Aguas Corrientes

The Palacio de Aguas Corrientes stands as one of Buenos Aires’ most extraordinary architectural landmarks, where public infrastructure was transformed into monumental art. Its richly decorated façade, vibrant ceramic details, and imposing presence reflect the ambition of a city determined to modernize with elegance and prestige.
